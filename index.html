Safety Tips

Simple habits can help reduce exposure to potentially unsafe websites.

Check the URL

Look carefully for misspellings, unusual domains and suspicious URL structures.

Don't Share Sensitive Data

Avoid entering passwords, payment details or personal information on unfamiliar websites.

Keep Software Updated

Keep your browser, operating system and security software updated with current security fixes.
